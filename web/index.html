CURB-65 and CRB-65

Adult community-acquired pneumonia severity assessment. Enter clinical observations to calculate the five-point CURB-65 score, or the four-point CRB-65 when urea/BUN is unavailable.

Clinical decision support only. The score does not diagnose pneumonia, exclude severe illness, or replace professional judgment. Hypoxemia, sepsis, comorbidities, and social circumstances may warrant urgent care regardless of the score.

Clinical observations

Urea >7 mmol/L or BUN >20 mg/dL scores one point. Check the reported units.

Criteria assessment

    These are severity scores, not individual mortality predictions or standalone treatment instructions.

    Scoring criteria

    • C — New-onset confusion
    • U — Urea >7 mmol/L (or BUN >20 mg/dL)
    • R — Respiratory rate ≥30/min
    • B — Systolic BP <90 mmHg or diastolic BP ≤60 mmHg
    • 65 — Age ≥65 years

    Each positive finding counts one point. CRB-65 omits urea; both versions are computed when a laboratory value is provided.

    Privacy: No patient names or identifiers are requested. Entered values are processed locally in your browser, without network submission or persistent storage.

    References: Lim WS et al. Thorax. 2003;58:377–382; ATS/IDSA adult CAP guidance. For education and professional reference, not autonomous triage.
